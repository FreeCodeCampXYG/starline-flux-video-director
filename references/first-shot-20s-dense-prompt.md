# 第一镜｜20 秒高密度导演提示词（v3）

本文用于 FLUX 3 Video 的第一个 t2v 镜头。写法吸收了参考教程中“先参数预览、再控制镜头、保持可延长和可衔接”的思路：先锁定不可变项，再按时间线定义动作与收束帧。它不是静态文生图提示词。

```text
FORMAT AND INTENT. Generate one single uninterrupted 20-second 16:9 cinematic technology-documentary opening about turning AI learning into a repeatable delivery system. This is not a trailer montage, music video, cyberpunk fantasy, or static portrait. The audience must understand without generated words: answers are abundant, human judgment is the scarce bottleneck. Apply an 80/20 directing rule: roughly 20 percent of the prompt locks the invariants (one person, room, light, lens, camera direction); roughly 80 percent specifies the visible timed event chain. Begin with momentum in the first second, build through legible causal changes, and finish on a stable continuity frame for the next shot. Keep one physical room, real-time take, camera move, human subject, and cause-and-effect chain. No cut, hidden cut, time lapse, teleportation, montage, reset, or sudden room transformation.

PRIMARY SUBJECT LOCK. One Chinese male independent developer, about thirty to thirty-four, short natural black hair, warm light-medium East Asian skin, thoughtful clean-shaven ordinary intelligent appearance, no glasses, headphones, jewelry, or brands. He wears one plain dark-charcoal long-sleeve overshirt and crew-neck dark undershirt; identical color, collar, sleeves and fabric for all twenty seconds, never a jacket, hoodie, suit, T-shirt, or futuristic costume. Stable age, hairstyle, face proportions and skin texture. Realistic anatomy: two arms, two hands, five fingers each, natural wrists, no duplicate body, extra limbs, or malformed fingers. His behavior is quiet and precise, never theatrical; emotional path: concentrated work, pressure, recognition, calm control.

ROOM AND GEOMETRY LOCK. Use one quiet modern home studio at night. The developer sits at a real dark walnut desk facing a single large monitor placed slightly to camera-right. The desk is broad and uncluttered: a compact keyboard centered in front of him, a small notebook near his left hand, one muted black desk speaker, no visible product logos, no readable screen content. Camera-left background contains a warm-gold articulated desk lamp behind the subject, casting a narrow warm rim light on his left shoulder and the desk edge. The monitor is the only deep-indigo key source and emits soft cool light across the front-right side of his face. Background remains the same throughout: dark shelving softly out of focus, a few neutral books and practical objects, dark wall, no windows changing light, no extra people. Maintain a controlled palette of deep indigo, charcoal black, soft walnut brown and restrained warm gold. The studio is believable and lived-in, not a glowing spaceship, not a neon cyberpunk set, not an office tower, not a bedroom, not a café. Keep the physical desk, monitor, lamp, chair, shelf and their relative positions fixed. The answer-card phenomenon comes only from the monitor and desk area; it must not fill the whole room with arbitrary objects.

CINEMATOGRAPHY LOCK. Premium live-action commercial documentary realism, 35mm spherical lens, 24 fps feeling, natural shutter motion, credible shallow depth, medium three-quarter opening. Camera just below eye line, about 1.5m from desk; one controlled forward dolly with slight right-to-left drift, ending medium close. Energetic foreground parallax, never shaky, dizzy, or face-blurring. No whip pan, crash zoom, Dutch angle, drone, orbit, or distortion. Cards may pass camera but his eyes and final gold question light remain readable. Realistic exposure: detailed shadows, no blown monitor, restrained non-neon gold.

ABSTRACT INFORMATION OBJECTS. Every AI answer card is a translucent thin glass-like rectangular panel with subtle frosted edges, neutral blue-gray translucency and tiny abstract nonlinguistic marks only. There must be no readable English, Chinese, code, numbers, logos, watermarks, icons that resemble brands, random letters, or fake UI. Cards are physically motivated: each begins as a faint pulse from the monitor, emerges forward, follows a clear curved trajectory through air, then slows or stacks in response to the bottleneck. They have slight mass, believable inertia, gentle air resistance and subtle reflected monitor light. They must not move like chaotic confetti or magical dust. The one verified card is visibly different only by a restrained warm-gold edge and a cleaner transparent center; it is not a trophy, passport, playing card, product ad or logo panel.

TWENTY-SECOND PERFORMANCE SCORE. 0.0 to 1.0 second: begin already in action. Camera is moving forward through two or three large translucent answer cards in extreme foreground; they pass from camera-left to camera-right with natural parallax. The developer is already seated, typing one final quiet keystroke, not frozen in an empty establishing shot. 1.0 to 3.5 seconds: the monitor emits a sequence of three visible cool-indigo pulses. Each pulse releases a small organized burst of answer cards. First burst passes above the keyboard, second burst curves behind his shoulder, third burst moves toward the lens. He notices the growing flow but keeps posture controlled. 3.5 to 6.0 seconds: the number of cards increases in three readable waves, not all at once. The first wave occupies the desk plane, the second builds a mid-air layer between monitor and camera, the third makes the foreground dense enough to create pressure. The camera continues the same measured dolly; do not change direction. 6.0 to 8.0 seconds: invisible workflow constraints become physically visible as the cards converge toward one narrow gap above the right edge of the keyboard, forming a clear bottleneck. Their speed decelerates naturally as they approach the gap; cards behind them queue in ordered layers. The developer stops typing, lifts his gaze from screen level toward the bottleneck, and rests both hands naturally on the desk.

8.0 to 10.0 seconds: make the core idea unmistakable without text. Almost all blue-gray answer cards reach the narrow gap and halt or gently stack; their edges catch faint cool light. A quiet gap opens in the visual noise around the developer's face. The camera is now close enough for natural facial detail, but do not force an extreme close-up. 10.0 to 12.0 seconds: from the exact center of the bottleneck, one compact warm-gold question-shaped abstract light forms through a physical mechanism: several gold path segments align, lock, and create one simple question-mark-like silhouette made of light, with no drawn typography and no floating punctuation character. It is an abstract symbolic light structure, not readable text. The developer's eyes settle on it. As it becomes sharp, only nearby cards gently fall out of focus; do not blur the entire image.

12.0 to 14.5 seconds: the question light releases one single narrow gold pulse back toward the monitor. This pulse travels along a visible path through the stacked cards, causing them to separate into a clean ordered corridor. The developer performs one small, deliberate right-hand movement: he turns his palm upward near the desk, not toward camera, as if ready to receive one relevant result. The pulse is restrained and physically coherent; no explosion, no particles filling the room, no supernatural energy beam.

14.5 to 16.5 seconds: one verified warm-gold-edged translucent card passes through the cleared bottleneck. It moves slowly but purposefully along the corridor, travels toward the developer's raised right hand, and decelerates just above his palm. All other answer cards remain stopped in quiet shallow-focus layers, visually proving that selection—not more generation—is the point. The camera maintains the same dolly and reaches its nearest position.

16.5 to 18.0 seconds: the verified card settles lightly above his palm without touching or changing his hand anatomy. The warm-gold question-shaped light remains centered in the line between his eyes and the card. The developer makes one nearly imperceptible breath and relaxed recognition, not a smile for camera. A soft warm-gold reflection appears on his cheek and fingertips. Keep his full face unobstructed.

18.0 to 20.0 seconds: resolve into the exact continuation frame. Stop all camera movement gradually, not abruptly. Hold a stable medium-close three-quarter composition: developer slightly left of center, his face visible, right hand and the hovering verified card in the lower center, one abstract warm-gold question light in the midground, the stopped queue of blue-gray answer cards forming depth behind it, monitor camera-right, warm lamp camera-left background. This final two-second hold must have clean geometry, no motion blur, no face obstruction, no changing expression, no new objects. It is designed to match-cut into a top-down task-map scene in the next shot.

SOUND INTENT. No voice or lyrics: quiet night-room tone, key tap, restrained monitor pulses, soft glass-air movement, one muted lock and calm confirmation chime. Add final narration and music in post.

HARD NEGATIVES. No readable text, letters, code, subtitles, fake UI, logos, watermark, branding, extra people, duplicate subject, face swap, costume or hairstyle changes, distorted face, malformed hands or extra fingers. No cartoon, anime, neon cyberpunk, magenta glow, explosions, smoke, glitter, random particles, shaky camera, flicker, jump cuts, montage, warped geometry, screen deformation, or objects passing through solids. Do not fade out or end black. Preserve one person, one room, one camera direction, one physical timeline, and the exact stable final continuity frame.
```

字符长度以 BFL Playground 的实时计数器为准；若超过限制，优先删除 `SOUND INTENT ONLY` 和负面约束中的重复词，绝不删改“主体锁定、场景几何、镜头锁定、20 秒时间轴、最终连续帧”。
